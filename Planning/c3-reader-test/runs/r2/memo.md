## 2. Scope: what was chosen and how it was found

### 2.1 The editors' headings were read first
[M-HEAD] `scripts/headings_dump.py` walked `volume_structures.structure_json` for all 553 volumes (`SELECT volume_id, structure_json FROM volume_structures ORDER BY volume_id`) into `out/headings.tsv`: 25,115 section rows. `scripts/doc_sections.py` mapped 314,616 documents to their deepest listing section (`out/doc_sections.tsv`). Headings were then scanned (Python `re`, lower-cased) for `recogni`, and separately for `revolution|coup|junta|provisional government|de facto|overthrow|golpe|military government|tobar|nonrecognition|non-recognition|diplomatic relations|insurrection|uprising|rebellion|seizure of power|unconstitutional` (`out/headings_recogn.tsv`, 253 rows; `out/headings_force.tsv`, 254 rows).
[M-HEAD-POST] `scripts/chapter_candidates.py` kept the 186 sections whose OWN title uses the recognition/force formula and whose documents classify as Latin American (`out/chapter_candidates.tsv`). The two latest are candidates 185 (frus1950v02, *Recognition by the United States of the military junta government of Haiti*, 1950) and 186 (frus1958-60v06, *U.S. interest in the Cuban revolution, the overthrow of the Batista government...*). No Latin American heading from 1950 on names recognition except candidate 185, and none names an overthrow except candidate 186. The post-1949 Latin American volumes' headings were listed in full and are country titles (*Political and economic relations of the United States and X*; from 1958, *U.S. relations with X*; from 1961, the country name alone).

### 2.2 Latin American classification
[M-LATAM] `scripts/latam_classify.py` (v4) walks each document's breadcrumb from the deepest heading upward: the first heading naming exactly one of the twenty republics (period spellings: Hayti, Santo Domingo, New Granada, Salvador, Argentine Republic, Mosquito/Bluefields for Nicaragua) assigns that country; a heading naming several republics counts as regional; a heading naming only Guyana, British Guiana, Jamaica, Bahamas, Eastern Caribbean, Suriname, Trinidad, Grenada, Barbados, Canada or Newfoundland excludes the document; inter-American headings or Latin-America-only volumes assign *Regional*; single-country volumes take the volume's country. Result: 61,412 Latin American documents (`out/latam_docs.tsv`), 1,824 excluded as non-republic. Known quirk: a heading like *...meeting at Montevideo, Uruguay* assigns Uruguay to a regional committee document (frus1943v05/d33); the country label is not used for any count in the overview.

### 2.3 Inclusion rule
[M-RULE] SCOPE = C ∪ T, apparatus excluded (`is_front_matter = 0 AND is_editorial_note = 0`).
- **C (editors' tier)**: every document directly listed in 51 curated sections whose own titles name recognition, non-recognition, withholding of recognition or continuance of relations for a Latin American government that took power by revolution, coup or other unconstitutional means. Chosen from the 186 candidates by reading each title (list in 2.4). Excluded as off-topic: meat-inspection labels, consuls, judgments, naturalization certificates, blockade, Monroe Doctrine, a constitution clause, protection of a citizen, Mexico's international personality, the 1896 Central American union, and the 1912 Haiti succession after Leconte's death. Mixed chapters whose titles bundle recognition with other matters (Mexico 1911–1916, Nicaragua 1911, Paraguay 1912, Haiti 1914–1915, Dominican Republic 1916, Costa Rica 1919, Argentina 1945, the 1930–1931 *Revolution in X* chapters, Cuba 1933) enter only through T.

### 3.1 Literal shares of the FTS rules (house rule (a)) and the referent test
[M-LS-T] T rule, hit list = the 1,348 Latin American non-apparatus hits sorted by (volume_id, document_id), evenly spaced sample of 40 (indices [0, 33, 67, 101, 134]... in `out/literal_share_T.json`). [M-REF] Referent test (a 1.000 tolerant share tests the stemmer, not the referent): the same 40 coded by reading header + KWIC (`out/referent_coding_T.tsv`): ON (recognition of a government that took power by force, or policy on it) **23 of 40**; RELATED (belligerency, or recognition between factions) 3 of 40; OFF (other senses of 'recognize') 14 of 40.
