Run directory: /Users/jbotts/frus-analysis/c3/runs/r9. Date: 2026-10-25. Index copy: /Users/jbotts/frus-analysis/c3/frus-copy-2026-10-25.db (read-only). Line labels (M1, M2 …) are the anchors the trace table at the end uses.

## M2. How the scope was found

- M2a. **The editors' headings, not my vocabulary.** `headings_scan.py` walked `SELECT volume_id, structure_json FROM volume_structures` (553 volumes) and matched section titles against `/recogni/i`: **253 headings** in the whole series (headings_recognition.tsv). Controls in the same pass: `ZZZ_IMPOSSIBLE_ZZZ` → 0 headings; `Department of State|United States` → 5,634 headings. Filtering those 253 to Latin American country names (grep in queries.log) gave the chapter list; a second pass for `Huerta|Madero|Carranza|de facto|junta|coup|provisional (president|government)` (103 headings, headings_defacto.tsv) added the Mexican Revolution chapters whose titles do not say "recognition". The 1876–1878 Mexico chapters were found by `frus_documents MATCH 'recogn* AND diaz'` over `frus1876`–`frus1878` (32 documents; headers all Foster–Fish–Evarts).
- M2b. **Heading practice ends about 1950.** The last Latin American recognition heading is `frus1950v02` "Haiti > Recognition by the United States of the military junta government of Haiti". After that the volumes are arranged by country and the question has to be found by full-text search.
- M2c. **Set A — chapter-defined, 1876–1950.** 74 chapters listed in setA_chapters.tsv (one row each: volume, first and last document id, mode, label), expanded by `expand_setA.py` from the structure JSON. Headings about something else that happened to match ("meat-inspection labels", "ship measurement certificates", "recognition of the Cuban consul-general", the 1896 political union, the 1919 Monroe Doctrine declaration, the 1923 Boston statement, the 1940 Panama constitution) were left out; the 1903 Panama circular is front matter and was left out. Mode **whole** keeps every non-apparatus document of a chapter (61 chapters, 1,543 documents); mode **fts** was used for the 13 large "political affairs" chapters (Mexico 1876–78, 1911, 1913, 1914, 1915, 1916; Haiti 1914, 1915; Dominican Republic 1914; Ecuador 1912; Peru 1913) and keeps only documents matching `frus_documents MATCH 'recogn*' AND volume_id=?` (288 of 2,216 chapter documents). **Set A = 1,831 (volume_id, document_id) pairs** (setA_pairs.tsv; per-chapter counts in expand_setA.out).
  - Literal share for the fts-mode pairs (`came_from_classes.py`): STRICT (`\b(recognition|recognizes?|recognized|recognizing|recognised|recognise)\b`, Python re, case-insensitive) **288 of 288**; TOLERANT (`recogni\w*`) **288 of 288**. A 1.000 share tests the stemmer, not the referent; the referent (recognition of a government) is tested by the chapter headings these documents sit under.
- M2d. **Set B — FTS-defined, 1950 and later.** Latin American volumes by manifest title: 49 (lavols.txt; the 1894 Nicaragua and 1902 Pious Fund appendices excluded). Query (setB_query.txt), over `frus_documents` joined to `document_cache` with `is_front_matter=0 AND is_editorial_note=0 AND volume_id IN (<49 ids>) AND volume_id >= 'frus1950'`:
  `NEAR(recogn* junta, 12) OR NEAR(recogn* coup, 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "new government", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "military government", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "de facto", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "revolutionary government", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "provisional government", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "new regime", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "military regime", 12) OR NEAR(recogn* "constitutional government", 12)` → **221 documents** in 22 volumes (setB_hits.tsv). Controls in the same volumes: `"Department of State"` → 8,797 documents in all 49 volumes; `ZZZ_IMPOSSIBLE_ZZZ` → 0.
  - Literal share (`setB_snips.py`, Python re, case-insensitive, over header+dateline+source_note+body_text whitespace-collapsed, the whole set of 221, not a sample): **STRICT 194 of 221** (a `recogni…` word within 12 words of one companion term spelled as in the query, single spaces); **TOLERANT 216 of 221** (companion terms with plurals and any run of space, hyphen, comma, parenthesis or full stop). The five tolerant misses are documents where "recogni" and the companion fall more than 12 words apart or where the stem matched a different word.
  - Hand classification (setB_classified.tsv, flag 1 = on the question; the list is setB_relevant.txt): **132 of 221** on the question. The 89 left out: the 1961 Bay of Pigs planning and the Cuban exile "provisional government" (recognition of an insurgent government, not of one in power), the 1950 China/Indochina and 1963 Saigon recognitions, Grenada (1979) and Suriname (1980), which are not Latin America, and generic uses of the verb ("it is recognized that…"). Each decision can be re-made from setB_snippets.txt, which holds up to four snippets per document.
- M2e. **Scope = Set A ∪ Set B(flagged) = 1,960 documents in 57 volumes** (scope_pairs.tsv; 1,828 A-only, 129 B-only, 3 in both: `frus1950v02` d476–d478). `scope_build.py`, scope_stats.txt. The scope is the inclusion rule's output, not a count of "recognition documents in FRUS": a document about recognition in a chapter the editors headed otherwise, after 1950 and outside the NEAR window, is not in it.
- M2f. **False-friend test.** `recogn*` alone measures the corpus, not the question: documents matching `recogn*` are **4,695 of 31,350** non-apparatus documents in the 49 Latin American volumes (15.0%) against **48,348 of 311,429** in the whole series (15.5%); the control phrase `"Department of State"` is 8,218 of 31,350 (26.2%) against 93,824 of 311,429 (30.1%) (false_friend.tsv). So no count in this memo rests on `recogn*` alone; the headings and the NEAR companions carry the referent.
- M2g. **Periodisation** is on `document_dates.date_iso` (the index's reading of `frus:doc-dateTime-min`, an instant at −05:00), never on the volume's series year. All 1,960 scope documents are dated. Denominators are my own query: `SELECT substr(dd.date_iso,1,3)||'0s', COUNT(*) FROM document_dates dd JOIN document_cache c ON c.volume_id=dd.volume_id AND c.document_id=dd.document_id WHERE c.is_front_matter=0 AND c.is_editorial_note=0 AND dd.date_iso IS NOT NULL AND c.volume_id NOT IN ('frus1951-54IranEd2','frus1969-76ve15p2Ed2') GROUP BY 1` (decade_denominators.tsv). ## M7. Decisions left to the historian (consequences measured where possible)

- "Came to power by force": Set A follows the editors' headings, which couple recognition with revolution, coup, forced resignation or assassination; it also holds cases the editors headed as recognition after a disputed election (Ecuador 1913, Peru 1919–20, Honduras 1919). Dropping those removes about 60 documents (the Peru 1919 chapter alone is 39).
- Brazil 1937 and Bolivia 1970/1978 are "no recognition question" cases, kept because the decision not to raise the question is itself the answer to the question asked.
- Recognition of an insurgent government not yet in power (Cuban exiles, 1961; the Sandinista junta before Somoza left) was excluded; adding the Cuban planning adds about 36 documents and changes the 1960s rate to 3.7 per 1,000.
- Pre-1876 cases were not read (M3b, M5d).
