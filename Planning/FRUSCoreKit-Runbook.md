# FRUSCoreKit runbook

What `FRUSCoreKit/` holds by area, which of the app's names forward to it, what each of FRUS Explorer
Light's phases moved into it or made public, and the seams a host passes in. `CLAUDE.md`'s
`FRUSCoreKit` entry, under *SPM package targets*, keeps the rules that bind every change to the kit;
this file holds the rest.

The paragraphs below were moved here from that entry word for word on 2026-11-26. There they stood as
one line of 48,003 characters, which every session and every lane's agent loaded with each
request. They are in the order they stood, each one line, each under a heading added in the move:
`grep -n '<TypeName>'` finds a paragraph, and `grep -n '^##'` lists the headings. A later paragraph
says where an earlier one's rule changed (the front-matter resolution, the semantic generator's
passes), so read to the end of an area before relying on its first paragraph.

**A move into the kit, or a declaration made public for a host, is recorded here**, in its area's
paragraph or in a new one at the end of *By area*, and not in `CLAUDE.md`. A rule that binds every
change to the kit goes in `CLAUDE.md`'s entry instead.

## The kit

### What the kit is, and what it depends on

`FRUSCoreKit` — the TEI parser, AST, render nodes, AST-to-render converter and HTML serializer, the reader's lookups and serializer settings (`ReaderRendering.swift`), the citation formatter, models, parser, canonical URL and page-span resolver, and (part 2) the indexing pipeline and the search service with the Foundation-only types they use, and Citation Lookup's matching engine, block splitter and page-range store with the lookup form's fields, the Add Documents citation line resolver and `CountCopy`: the code FRUS Explorer Light, the web edition, compiles on Linux from a pinned commit. It depends on SourceNoteKit, FTS5Store, since FRUS Explorer Light's S21a TEIHeaderKit, since its S26a SemanticVectorsKit, and since its S29b WordCloudKit.

### Foundation only: the guarded imports

**Foundation only**: FoundationXML, FoundationNetworking (Linux's `URLSession`, since FRUS Explorer Light's S22c), CryptoKit (swift-crypto's `Crypto` on Linux), OSLog, SQLite3 (the web edition's `CSQLite` on Linux), FTS5Store, SourceNoteKit, TEIHeaderKit, since FRUS Explorer Light's S24c CoreGraphics (whose geometry types Linux's Foundation declares itself), since its S26a SemanticVectorsKit and since its S29b WordCloudKit are imported behind `canImport`, and the stand-ins under `Linux/` (`LinuxFoundationShims.swift`, `LinuxOSLogShims.swift`) compile to nothing on Apple platforms.

### What the indexing pipeline is given: the seams

The pipeline is given what the app reads: `IndexingResources` (the bundled data files, as providers), an `IndexingStampStore` (whose requirements are `UserDefaults`' own methods) and an optional `IndexedDocumentDonor`; the app's half — `IndexingResources.bundled`, `extension UserDefaults: IndexingStampStore`, the Spotlight donor and rebuild, `updateSummary(_:)` and the initialiser every app call site uses — is in `FRUSExplorer/Search/IndexingPipeline+App.swift`, and `runPostIndexPasses` runs the passes after indexing: the app's launch calls it, and the web edition's indexer is to.

### The matching engine's volume catalogue

The matching engine reads the volumes it answers for from a `CitableVolumeCatalogue`; the app's is `ManifestStore`, conformed beside it, and a host outside the app passes a `FixedVolumeCatalogue`.

### Declarations move into the kit: the forwarders, and the stores that stay in the app

A declaration the kit needs that lives in an Apple-only file moves INTO the kit, never the reverse, and the app keeps the old name as a forwarder or typealias (`FRUSURLSchemeHandler.resolveCrossRefTarget` → `FRUSURLScheme`, `DocumentHighlight.Color` → `HighlightColor`, `DocumentHighlight.HighlightDTO` → `ReaderHighlight`, `HighlightReview.locate` → `HighlightReanchor`, `IndexingPipeline.normalizeSourceNoteWrapper` → `StoredSourceNote`, `NARACatalogClient.isDecimalFileNumber` → `DecimalFileSegment`, `WorkingCorpus.CaptureTruncation` → `CorpusCaptureTruncation`, `SearchHistoryEntry.RecordedCount` → `RecordedSearch.RecordedCount`, `CorporaAxis.truncationLine` → `CorpusCaptureTruncation.line`, `PinnedResultBase.quotable` → `QueryQuoting.quotable`, `ResultSetScope.isMeaningCaptureProvenance` → `WorkingCorpusProvenance.isMeaningCapture`, `DownloadManager.gitBlobSHA1` → `VolumeUpdateChecker.gitBlobSHA1`, `HighlightReview.status(of:currentVersion:)` → `HighlightReview.status(renderingVersion:selectedText:currentVersion:)`, `AnalyticsView.sourceColoring` → `AnalyticsSourceColoring.sourceColoring(_:seriesCount:)`, `ChronologyViewModel`'s statics and constants → `ChronologyGrouping`, `PersonCoMentionGraphViewModel.partnerLimit`, `partners`, `capApplied` and `capDisclosure` → `PersonCoMentionNetwork`); the kit never reads `Bundle.main` or `UserDefaults` (`BrokenRefsIndexStore`, `ResolvedEdgeIndexStore`, `CitedPageMapStore`, `ManifestStore`, `CitationStyle.current` and the loaders `DocumentSubjectStore`, `DecimalClassLabelStore`, `VolumeSubjectProfilesStore` and `PersonAuthorityIndexStore` stay in the app, with Source Explorer's eleven stores, and About the Series' `SourceProvenanceStore`, `AdministrationProfilesStore` and `VolumeLevelTagStore`).
