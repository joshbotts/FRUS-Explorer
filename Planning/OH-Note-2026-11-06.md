### 1. Papers filed inside the paper before them: `frus1870` and 43 more volumes

**Confirmed.** `frus1870` shows it plainest. The volume's own List of Papers (line 4201) numbers 303 papers, 1 to 303, none twice. The file holds 300 documents, `d1` to `d300`. Each of the three papers missing from it is inside the document before it, as a `frus:attachment`:

| Paper, as the List of Papers gives it | Its row | Filed inside | Its head, and the date its opener prints |
|---|---|---|---|
| No. 216, Baron Gerolt to Mr. Fish, Feb. 19, p. 329 | line 6470 | `d215` (line 27794), No. 215, Mr. Fish to Mr. Low, Dec. 3, p. 303 | line 30237, "February 19, 1870" |
| No. 284, Mr. De Long to Mr. Fish, Jan. 22, p. 460 | line 7248 | `d282` (line 40063), No. 283, Mr. De Long to Mr. Fish, Jan. 17, p. 455 | line 40555, "January 22, 1870" |
| No. 303, Mr. Schuyler to Mr. Fish, Aug. 31, p. 521 | line 7488 | `d300` (line 45611), No. 302, Mr. Schuyler to Mr. Fish, July 17, p. 520 | line 45645, "August 31, 1870" |

**Why each is a paper of its own**

- The List of Papers gives each its own number, writer, date, subject and page.
- Each is dated after the paper it is filed in:
  - Baron Gerolt's letter of February 19, 1870 sits inside Mr. Fish's instruction of December 3, 1869.
  - Mr. De Long's despatch of January 22 sits inside his of January 17.
  - Mr. Schuyler's despatch No. 66 of August 31 sits inside his of July 17.

**What follows from it**

- **The printed numbers shift against `@n`.** In this volume a document's first source note is its paper number: `d3` opens with `<note rend="inline" type="source">No. 3.</note>` (line 7796). 295 documents open with such a number, and in 293 it is the paper's number in the List of Papers:
  - equal to `@n` up to `d215`;
  - `@n` + 1 from `d216` ("No. 217.");
  - `@n` + 2 from `d283` ("No. 285."), to `d300`'s "No. 302.".

  For 258 of the 293 the List of Papers gives the document's own date. Of the 17 that differ, 16 are item 5's misencoded dates, and the list gives `d45` July 19 against its dateline's July 18. The other 18 rows give no date in the form "Feb. 19".
- **So every document from `d216` on is addressed one or two below the paper it prints.** `frus1870/d250` is printed paper No. 251.
- **This is the error #25 described in 2016:** new documents taken for attachments of the one before, where a document's start was not clear. `frus1870` was not among #25's 52 volumes; its own checklist is #223 (2018). In this volume the paper number is the source note that opens each document, and none of the three papers carries its own.
- **Moving each into a `div type="document"` of its own renumbers the 85 documents after `d215`,** as #25's corrections renumbered theirs.

**Three slips the List of Papers settles**

- **`d131`** (line 19064). Its source note reads "No. 92.]" (line 19065): Mr. De Long's own despatch number, in the form the openers print. The paper's number, 131, is nowhere in the document. The List of Papers gives No. 131 as Mr. De Long to Mr. Fish, Oct. 10, the date of `d131`'s opener.
- **`d230`** (line 34902). Its source note reads "No. 23." (line 34903). The paper is No. 231, of Sept. 26, and the document's opener reads "No. 31.] Legation of the United States, Pekin, September 26, 1870".
- **No. 284's head** (line 40555) reads "Mr. Be Long" for "Mr. De Long".

### 2. `frus1977-80v27`: two lists that no document links to

**Confirmed.**

- **The lists.**
  - "Persons" (`xml:id="persons"`, line 871) holds 153 `persName/@xml:id`.
  - "Abbreviations and Terms" (`xml:id="terms"`, line 482) holds 143 `term/@xml:id`.
- **No document links to them.** None of the volume's 100 documents carries a `persName/@corresp`, a `gloss/@target` or a document-level `@ana`. Its 30 tagged names all sit in a `signed`, with no `@corresp`.
- **Every other volume with such lists links them.**
  - Of the other 282 volumes whose List of Persons has entries, 281 link it inline, and `frus1981-88v16` points to it by `@ana` (item 3).
  - Of the other 312 with a List of Abbreviations, 311 link it inline, and `frus1981-88v16` points to it by `@ana`.
  - In the same subseries, `frus1977-80v26` links a name in 485 of its 501 documents (5,560 links) and a term in 497 (10,971).
- **Both lists came with the commit that published the volume,** `e008aee30` ("fix: publish frus1977-80v27", 2025-08-21). No later commit adds a link.
- **Neither division carries `subtype="index"`,** which `schema/add-front-matter-section-subtype.xsl` gives a front-matter `persons` or `terms` division (line 81). The only other volume whose lists lack it, `frus1969-76ve09p2`, links them from 223 and 226 of its 231 documents.
- **The checks pass it.** `schema/frus-ids.sch` tests that each `persName/@corresp` resolves (line 173). Nothing tests that a listed person is cited, so the volume passes.

### 3. `frus1981-88v16`: people and terms named only by `@ana`

**Question.**

- **The lists.** "Persons" (line 741) holds 120 ids and "Abbreviations and Terms" (line 450) holds 108, both `subtype="index"`.
- **No inline links.** No document carries a `persName/@corresp` or a `gloss/@target`. Its 47 tagged names all sit in a `signed`.
- **Every document carries `@ana` instead.** All 88 documents name their people and terms on the document's own `@ana`: 1,041 pointers, each to an id in the two lists (622 persons, 419 terms). The first is `d368` (line 3813):

  ```
  ana="#p_ARVD_1 #p_BF_1 #p_BJA_1 #p_FRW_1 #p_HAMJ_1 #p_RRW_1 #p_VAB_1 #t_EXDIS_1 #t_OAS_1 #t_SS_1 #t_SSO_1 #t_USG_1"
  ```

- **It is the only volume that does this.** None of the other 552 volumes does. The pointers came with `5b6aa8608` ("fix: apply persons & terms, finish xref enrichment", 2026-09-14), which added `@ana` to all 88 documents and no inline link.

**The question.** Is a document-level `@ana` how volumes will name their people and terms from now on? Or will inline `persName/@corresp` and `gloss/@target` follow, perhaps as the rest of the volume is published (its `revisionDesc` reads `partially-published`)? FRUS Explorer reads both kinds of pointer, and lists each document's people and terms below it. The answer tells us whether to expect more volumes to work this way.

In these volumes, a number standing alone at the head of a document is a `note[@type="source"]` instead:

- **`frus1870`:** each document's paper number, as in item 1.

## B.3 What the check found in the app

- **`frus1941-43` reads as a List of Persons that nothing links to.**
  - Its persons view lists six of the index's entries as people ("Persons (6)", among them "Meetings" and "Views on"), the People browser lists them among everyone's with no mention each, and the axis's People tagged list marks it with the amber line "Prints a List of Persons, but none of its documents links a name to it." Neither is true of an index. (This bullet first said the volume's page reads "6 people listed". It does not: the page's People row shows only for a volume that carries the layer.)
  - The cause is in the persons-list parser. With no ids it uses the entry's text as its key (`FRUSDocumentParser`: `let id = currentId ?? raw`), and 6 of the 350 entries pass `PersonListHeuristics.isLikelyPersonName`.
  - `VolumeAnnotationsIndexGenerator` counts the index's `persons` rows, so `volume-annotations-index.json` holds `personsListed` 6.
  - `AnnotationsAxisTests.theShippedFile` expects the mark (line 254: the "without" drill's notes are `frus1941-43`'s and `frus1977-80v27`'s), so the fix changes that expectation too.
- **`frus1981-88v16` reads as a List of Abbreviations that nothing links to.**
  - Its Abbreviations row carries "Prints a List of Abbreviations, but none of its documents links a term to it."
  - In fact its 88 documents point at its terms 419 times through `@ana`. The index has read the person pointers since v67 (#1599), but not the term pointers.
- **`frus1870`'s numbers do no harm in the app,** but its addresses do.
  - The app reads a despatch number only from a `seg` (`IndexingPipeline.extractDespatchSerial`), so it takes no paper number for one. `d131`'s "No. 92.]" sits in a source note and is not read.
  - From `d216` on, the app, like history.state.gov, addresses each document one or two below its printed number. That is fixed upstream or not at all.

The first two are fixed, at index version 74 (plan of record decision 119; the design's §11, after lane D):
- **`frus1941-43`, in the parser,** the owner's choice over the generator alone. A `<list type="index">` opens no person, and its division is an `index` in the outline, which Browse opens as printed text.
- **`frus1981-88v16`, in the generator,** with no index version of its own. This note said reading the term pointers would take one, as #1599 did for the people. It does not: the index stores no term mention for any volume, and the Abbreviations count is the generator's TEI scan, which now reads `@ana` as the reader's lists do.
