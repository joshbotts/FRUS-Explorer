# C-3 reader-facing tier — what the question should give each reading, written before the first cell

Written 2026-10-31, before calibration, the canary or any cell, so that the result can contradict it
(`Planning/C-3-Reader-Facing-Tier-Design.md` §1). The figures are this session's, from C-3's index copy
(version 71) read-only and the bundle's Tier-1 binary at digest `89481b18…`; the assessment's scripts and
outputs were in the session's scratchpad and were deleted before the first cell. The question is the
owner's, chosen 2026-10-30 from four unused candidates.

**The properties (design §1).**
- **Not used, printed nowhere.** "soft power", "messaging strategies" and "regional audience" occur in no
  file under `Planning/` or `Docs/`. "Public diplomacy" occurs in 33, all incidental: the subseries'
  name in volume lists, a semantic-search evaluation's query set, and C-2's preamble tables.
- **Its own term is absent from the corpus.** `"soft power"` returns no document. So every session has to
  translate the concept into the period's words, and every session has a zero to report.
- **The scope.** A family of the period's words (propaganda, psychological warfare and strategy, the
  information program and activities, USIA and USIS, public diplomacy, cultural relations and exchange,
  the Voice of America, American prestige) matches about 22,300 ordinary documents in all volumes. About
  6,300 of them are in the 102 volumes whose titles name the region or one of its countries, and about 490
  in the four Public Diplomacy volumes the copy holds. A session will have to narrow it: the largest
  family, propaganda, alone matches about 12,100.
- **Eras.** In the region's volumes, by document date: about 320 in the 1930s, 1,780 in the 1940s, 2,100
  in the 1950s, 1,570 in the 1960s, 550 in the 1970s. The Public Diplomacy volumes add the 1910s (27) and
  the 1960s and 1970s.
- **Stems shorter than common forms.** `persuasiv*` reaches no stem; `psychologic*`, `credibilit*` and
  `communicat*` reach far fewer stems than their shorter forms; `information` is indexed as `inform`.
- **Both channels.** Came-from rows for about 6,260 of the region scope's documents; pointed-at rows
  (about 2,470) for about 1,430 of them.
- **Before 1940.** About 2,080 matching documents in 161 volumes, 320 of them in the region's volumes.
- **Room for the vectors.** For three anchors per era from the 1930s to the 1970s (15 in all), 137 of
  their 375 nearest neighbours lie outside the word scope and carry none of the family's words: about
  one in three, against about three in four on R16's corruption question. Most are same-country
  political reporting.

**What the arms should show.**
- **W, the writing.** The pilot's effects should hold: W's median prose sentence 22 words or fewer, part 1
  within 200 words, a Documents cited list that resolves the text's short citations, part 2 as two lists,
  and the judge ranking W above A in at least three blocks of four. The question invites many numbers
  (decades, channels, families), so O2' is the reading most likely to fail; expected: W's untraced
  numerals fewer than A's, and O2' in two or three of W's four runs.
- **W, the vocabulary.** V8 obeyed in 4 of 4 W runs, and violated in most A and K runs, as R16's A was.
- **K, the stems.** The family invites prefixes written past their stems. Expected: at least one A run
  writes a dead term and does not notice it; K looks the stem up before its prefixes in every run that
  writes one.
- **K, the zeros.** Every run reports that "soft power" returns nothing. Expected: K prints beside it a
  relaxed form of the same query with its count in 4 of 4 runs; A prints a positive control, a different
  query, instead.
- **K, the guide's figures.** The pre-war scope is real, so most runs will speak to the pre-war archival
  roadmap. Expected: A runs restate "11 of 695" as the block prints it, as three of R16's four A runs did;
  K runs re-derive their figure or attribute it.
- **The cost.** Neither W nor K loses more than one item of the 35 beyond A's.
- **Reach.** Less room than on corruption: at most one strict vector-reached document a run.

**What would contradict this.** The judge ranking W above A in fewer than three blocks; W's sentences no
shorter than A's; K's zeros printed with no relaxed form beside them; K writing prefixes it never looked
up; either treatment losing two or more items of the 35 beyond A's.
