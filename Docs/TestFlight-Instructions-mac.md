# What's New in Build 33 (Mac)

Build 33 **redesigns the document reading experience**, changes how documents open across windows, and adds tools for finding the *next* document. No re-index this build — everything works on your existing library.

## The Research rail (redesigned)
Everything you do *with* a document — cite, summarize, note, tag, highlight, collect, and explore its sources, cross-references, and related documents — now lives in one **trailing Research rail**. The always-on research strip is gone; the **⌘⇧R** toggle in the titlebar shows or hides the rail beside the document (it overlays the text when the window is narrow).

- **Inside the rail:** a tile grid — **Cite · Word Cloud · Sources · Graph · Related · Share** — over expandable **Summary · Notes · Tags · Collections** sections.
- **Slimmer titlebar:** Search and Browse as icons, **Analytics** and **My Research** as labelled menus, and the rail toggle. The centred document-id pill yields before those tools as the window narrows.

## The floating selection bar
**Select any text** and a small bar appears at the selection: four **colour dots** (click to highlight), **Excerpt** (into a collection), **Look Up** (NARA), and **Note**. Highlighting no longer needs a mode or a colour-picker popover.

## Documents open where you're working
When you open a document from a **tool window** — Search, the cross-reference graph, the corpus browser, Related Documents — it now lands in the window you launched that tool *from*, not whichever window happens to be frontmost. Open a second main window (**⌘N**) and its tools route to it independently. Use **Open in New Window** (a search result's context menu, or **File ▸ Open Document in New Window**) to deliberately spin off a fresh document window.

## Read mode
Hide the rail for distraction-free reading; **hover the left/right edges** of the document for previous/next chevrons. Set your default — **Read / Research / Remember last** — in **Settings → Reading**.

## Finding the next document
- **Related Documents** — the rail's **Related** tile opens a ranked reading list (**archival provenance, cross-references, date, shared people**) in its own window that stays open beside the document as you step through results. Tunable **weight sliders** and a **This volume / subseries / All volumes** scope.
- **Custom Volume Scopes** — name a set of volumes once (**Settings → Research → Volume Scopes**) and reuse it in Search, Analytics, and Word Clouds, with honest **"N of M indexed"** counts.
- **Detected-topic filters (experimental)** — each volume's **Top subjects** become filters in Search and Analytics. Auto-detected, not editorial — report mistags.
- **Source Explorer** — resolved collections show the **HMS/MLR Entry** number NARA staff ask for.
- **Cross-reference graph** — scroll-wheel zoom and right-click node menus (Recenter, Open in Main Window, Archival Neighbors).
- **Zotero** — connect in **Settings → Integrations**, then **Share** a document (with tags and notes) to your library.

## Fixes
- **Research window** — fixed a list that could reorder/jump just after opening.
- **Windows** — launching a tool whose window is already open brings it to the front; tool windows restore correctly across relaunch.

## Feedback
The big ones: the **reading redesign** (does the rail put things where you'd look, is **Read mode** calmer?) and the new **window routing** — launch a tool from one window, glance at another, then open a result, and confirm it opens **where you started**; tell us any misroute. Then stress **Related Documents** and **Volume Scopes**. Include your macOS version, the volume/document number, what you clicked, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots and crash reports help. Thanks for testing!
